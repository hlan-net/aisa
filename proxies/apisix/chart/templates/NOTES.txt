The APISIX proxy of aisa is installed. Applications call it as an OpenAI-compatible API:

  OPENAI_BASE_URL=http://{{ include "proxy.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc{{ if ne (int .Values.service.port) 80 }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}{{ end }}/v1

It asks aisa at {{ include "proxy.aisaURL" . }} for every request.

How an application uses it, its credential and the errors it can get:
https://github.com/hlan-net/aisa/blob/main/docs/features/client-integration.md
